Fedora Drive Sync privacy policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026.

Personal use and purpose

Fedora Drive Sync is a personal backup setup operated by the owner of the connected Google account. It uses rclone on that owner's Fedora Linux computer to back up selected local folders to Google Drive.

Access and use of Google data

The setup requests Google Drive access that permits reading file contents and metadata, listing folders, creating folders, uploading files, updating existing files, and deleting files. Its configured backup commands use this access to compare selected local folders with their Drive destinations and transfer new or changed files. If mirror mode is enabled, destination files removed locally can also be deleted.

Storage

Source files are stored on the owner's computer and uploaded copies are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the rclone configuration. Local operational logs may contain filenames, paths, and transfer errors.

Sharing and other uses

The backup setup transfers data between the owner's computer and Google Drive. It does not send backup files or credentials to this public website or to a separate developer-operated service. Google processes the uploaded data under its own terms and policies. The setup does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or AI model training.

Retention and owner control

Backup files remain in Google Drive until the owner removes them or configured mirror operations delete them. Google's trash and retention rules apply. The owner can stop the local backup job, remove its local credentials and logs, delete stored backups, or revoke access through the Google Account's third-party connections settings.

Google API user data

This setup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

The operator is the owner of the connected Google account. The support email is the email selected in the app's Google authorization settings.
